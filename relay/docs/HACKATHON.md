## Suggested project writeup

**Problem:** During a flood, scattered community reports can describe the same incident, while responders may be assigned without checking skills or current availability.

**Build:** Relay is a local response rehearsal with a 3D command centre. A plain-language report becomes an editable triage draft. Coordinators can combine possible duplicate reports while preserving evidence. Matching checks skill, capacity, district and availability; an explicit human confirmation precedes assignment. An incident timeline and decision log explain what happened. Synthetic injection and isolated replay make the workflow demonstrable.

**AWS:** The real AWS Cedar open-source WebAssembly engine evaluates report, fusion, dispatch and resolution policies. Its Allow/Deny results and policy IDs are visible in the product. Roles are simulated, and browser evaluation is not server-enforced security. AWS cloud services and model-based AI are not connected.

**Learning:** Replace this paragraph with your own account of what you learned and can explain: Cedar permit/forbid behavior, matching constraints, provenance-preserving fusion, or Three.js interaction and motion accessibility. Do not present generated claims as personal experience.

**AI assistance:** Substantial design and coding assistance from OpenAI Codex. Dependencies and inspiration are credited in `NOTICE.md`.
